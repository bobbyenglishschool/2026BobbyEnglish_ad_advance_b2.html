💡 教學理念：關於這個階段的英語學習 | Educational Philosophy

國中二、三年級的學生，在英語學習上常面臨關鍵的轉折點：單字量逐漸增加，卻缺少在生活中深度探討議題的機會；文法規律雖然理解，但在寫作表達時仍習慣依賴單純的短句。

Students in their 8th and 9th grades often reach a critical turning point in English learning. While their vocabulary expands, they rarely have the opportunity to discuss complex topics in depth, and their writing often relies on simple sentences despite understanding grammar rules.

本課程適合目前就讀國二或國三，在學校基礎課程之外，希望能將英語作為知識理解與深度表達工具的同學。

This course is suitable for 8th and 9th graders who, beyond their standard school curriculum, wish to use English as a tool for academic understanding and deeper expression.

這個階段的學習，我們著重在閱讀視野與語法結構的並進。透過接觸科學、歷史與藝術等跨學科文本，引導同學習慣多角度思考；同時，我們藉由系統化的文法梳理與課堂即席寫作，協助孩子將零碎的短句，逐步鍛鍊為結構嚴謹、具邏輯性的長編段落，讓英語表達從單純的日常對話，內化為清晰的思辨能力。

At this stage, we focus on advancing both reading horizons and grammatical structures simultaneously. By exploring interdisciplinary texts in science, history, and art, we guide students to think from multiple perspectives. Meanwhile, through systematic grammar review and in-class writing, we assist students in developing fragmented sentences into rigorously structured, logical paragraphs, transforming simple conversation into clear, critical expression.

📅 34堂課：學期進度藍圖 | 34-Lesson Curriculum Blueprint

本課程為每週2堂，每堂2小時。每堂課時間分配：60分鐘核心閱讀討論、40分鐘系統文法解構、20分鐘課堂主題寫作。
2 classes per week, 2 hours per class. Each session allocates 60 mins for core reading, 40 mins for grammar deconstruction, and 20 mins for thematic writing.

  • 第一階段：時態深化與未來預測 (Lessons 1-10)
    • 跨學科主題 (Discover)：探討地球環境變遷與太空旅行的未來。Academic Themes (Discover): Exploring Earth's environmental changes and the future of space travel.
    • 文法與寫作 (Azar)：進階時態比較，練習短文寫作「我心目中的 2050 年太空旅行」。Grammar & Writing (Azar): Comparison of advanced tenses; paragraph practice on "My Vision of Space Travel in 2050."
  • 第二階段：進階時態與動詞變化 (Lessons 11-20)
    • 跨學科主題 (Discover)：藝術的創新形式與科學上的偶發發現。Academic Themes (Discover): Innovative forms of art and accidental scientific discoveries.
    • 文法與寫作 (Azar)：現在完成式與被動語態，練習短文寫作「影響我最深的一項科學發明」。Grammar & Writing (Azar): Perfect tenses and passive voice; paragraph practice on "An Invention That Influenced Me Most."
  • 第三階段：長句拆解與高級子句 (Lessons 21-30)
    • 跨學科主題 (Discover)：古文明歷史遺跡與食品健康科學。Academic Themes (Discover): Ancient historical ruins and the science of food and health.
    • 文法與寫作 (Azar)：三大子句（名詞、形容詞、副詞子句），練習短文寫作「如果我是古文明的考古學家」。Grammar & Writing (Azar): Mastery of noun, adjective, and adverb clauses; paragraph practice on "If I Were an Ancient Archaeologist."
  • 第四階段：綜合複習與成果展現 (Lessons 31-34)
    • 文法與寫作 (Azar)：國中全範圍核心文法綜合演練，並進行期末主題小組報告分享。Grammar & Writing (Azar): Comprehensive review of junior high core grammar, concluding with final group presentations.

🏆 本學期五大核心學習目標 | Five Core Learning Objectives

  • 1. 完整掌握核心文法：融會貫通完成式、被動語態與複合子句等複雜句型。
    Core Grammar Mastery: Thoroughly integrate perfect tenses, passive voice, and complex clauses.
  • 2. 理解 6 個跨學科主題：完成 Discover 4 前 12 單元的閱讀，累積厚實的學科詞彙。
    6 Interdisciplinary Themes: Complete the first 12 units of Discover 4 and build a solid academic vocabulary.
  • 3. 具備 100-150 字獨立寫作能力：配合文法進度，掌握原因分析與敘事文等基本文體結構。
    Paragraph Essay Writing: Write 100-150 word paragraphs, mastering cause-and-effect and narrative structures.
  • 4. 建立英語討論習慣：在 10 人以內的課堂環境中，獲得充分的口說表達與同儕互動機會。
    Habitual English Discussion: Ensure ample opportunities for oral expression and peer interaction within a small class under 10 students.
  • 5. 奠定長期語言能力基礎：在完成階段性升學文法目標的同時，提早適應高中與國際檢定所需的閱讀深度。
    Long-term Language Foundation: Meet current academic standards while adapting to the reading depth required for high school and international exams.

📋 學習支持與請假配套 | Learning Support & Attendance Policy

為確保每位同學的學習完整性與進度銜接，若因故不克出席且無法安排實體補課，我們提供以下細緻的進度支持方案：
To ensure learning continuity and progress tracking, the following support plans are available if a student is unable to attend and an in-person make-up class cannot be scheduled:

  • 課堂重點筆記與進度指引：教師將於課後提供當堂課的核心文法圖解筆記、寫作關鍵字架構，以及 Discover 4 的討論主題摘要，讓孩子在家中也能精準掌握課程核心。
    Class Notes & Guided Study: Comprehensive grammar summaries, writing frameworks, and discussion outlines from the missed session will be provided to help the student review at home.
  • 個別化寫作點評：請假同學仍可於期限內提交當堂課的「課堂現場寫作」與文法練習，授課老師將進行一對一的細緻批改與反饋，確保寫作邏輯不落後。
    Individual Essay Feedback: Students can still submit their in-class paragraph writing and grammar exercises. The instructor will provide one-on-one detailed corrections and feedback.
  • 課前/課後 10 分鐘重點釋疑：於下次實體上課時，授課老師將利用課前或課後時間，單獨為孩子進行 10 分鐘的核心觀念檢視與答疑，協助孩子安心銜接新進度。
    Brief Check-in Sessions: The instructor will arrange a 10-minute brief consultation before or after the next class to clarify key concepts and ensure a smooth transition to the next unit.
